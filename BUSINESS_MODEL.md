# 💰 Aether Labs Business Model & Monetization Strategy

**Goal:** Build a sustainable, high-impact AI company that generates significant revenue while advancing open scientific discovery.

## Core Philosophy
We believe the fastest path to both scientific progress *and* profitability is a **hybrid open-core + premium SaaS** model.

- Keep foundational research and core models open source (builds reputation, attracts talent, accelerates the field).
- Monetize premium access, hosted infrastructure, enterprise features, and specialized services.

## Primary Revenue Streams (2026–2027 Focus)

### 1. SaaS Platform – Aether Research (Main Revenue Engine)
**Product:** Intelligent AI research platform for scientists and R&D teams.

**Key Features (MVP + v1):**
- Advanced scientific literature synthesis & reasoning
- Hypothesis generation & prioritization
- Multi-step scientific simulation & planning
- Private project workspaces
- API access for integration
- Collaboration & sharing tools

**Pricing Tiers (Proposed):**

| Tier          | Price          | Target Users                  | Key Benefits                              | Status      |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------|
| Free          | $0             | Individual researchers, students | Limited queries, public projects          | MVP target  |
| Pro           | $29/user/mo    | Serious researchers & small labs | Higher limits, private projects, exports, priority | Beta        |
| Team          | $99+/mo        | Research groups & startups    | Shared workspaces, admin controls, SSO    | Planned     |
| Enterprise    | Custom         | Pharma, Energy, Govt labs     | Dedicated instances, custom models, SLA, on-prem options | Future      |

**Go-to-Market:**
- Start with academic & indie researchers (content + community)
- Expand to biotech, materials science, energy R&D teams
- Leverage GitHub, arXiv, conferences, and researcher networks

**Target ARR (Year 1–2):** $500k – $2M+ from subscriptions + usage

### 2. Enterprise Research Partnerships & Custom Development
High-margin engagements where we build or fine-tune specialized AI systems for large organizations.

Examples:
- Custom hypothesis engines for drug discovery
- Materials simulation copilots
- Internal knowledge + reasoning platforms

**Pricing:** Project-based ($50k–$500k+) or retainer

### 3. Premium Hosting, Fine-Tuning & API
Even if core models are open, many organizations want managed, secure, high-performance hosting + domain-specific fine-tuning.

### 4. Grants, Sponsored Research & Non-Dilutive Funding
Actively pursue NSF, DOE, NASA, DARPA, and corporate sponsored research. This funds core R&D without giving up equity.

## Unit Economics Targets (Year 2+)
- Gross margin on SaaS: >75%
- LTV:CAC > 3:1
- Payback period < 12 months

## Competitive Moat
- Deep scientific specialization (not general chatbots)
- Open research reputation + community
- Data flywheel from platform usage (with privacy)
- Talent density in scientific AI

## 2026 Revenue Milestones
- Q2: Company foundation + positioning
- Q3: MVP launch (Free tier) + first 100 users
- Q4: Pro tier launch + first paying customers ($10k+ MRR target)

## Why This Works
This model lets us:
- Attract top talent and contributors through openness
- Generate real revenue quickly from the people who benefit most (professional researchers & labs)
- Maintain mission alignment (advancing science for humanity)
- Build a defensible, high-margin business

**We are not building another generic LLM wrapper. We are building the operating system for scientific discovery — and charging the organizations that will pay the most for it.**
