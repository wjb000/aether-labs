**Aether Labs** is building the next generation of AI systems designed to accelerate humanity's quest to understand the fundamental nature of reality.

We are a research-driven company focused on creating AI that doesn't just answer questions — it *asks better ones*, simulates complex systems, generates novel hypotheses, and collaborates with scientists across disciplines.

## Our Mission

To develop safe, powerful, and open AI technologies that empower researchers, explorers, and dreamers to push the boundaries of human knowledge — from the quantum realm to the cosmic web.

## Our Vision

A world where AI is a trusted co-pilot in scientific discovery. Where breakthroughs in physics, biology, chemistry, and cosmology happen faster because humans and machines think together.

We envision Aether as the "operating system for scientific curiosity."

## Core Principles

- **Truth-Seeking**: We prioritize understanding over hype. Rigorous science first.
- **Openness**: Core research and tools will be open source whenever possible.
- **Human-AI Symbiosis**: AI amplifies human ingenuity, never replaces the wonder.
- **Long-Term Impact**: Building for decades, not quarters.
- **Safety & Alignment**: Advanced AI must be developed responsibly.

## Product & Business Model

**First Product:** Aether Research Platform

An AI-powered scientific discovery platform (SaaS + API) that helps researchers synthesize literature, generate hypotheses, run simulations, and collaborate.

**Monetization:** Hybrid open-core + premium SaaS (Free / Pro / Team / Enterprise tiers). See [BUSINESS_MODEL.md](BUSINESS_MODEL.md) for full strategy.

We will generate revenue from subscriptions, enterprise partnerships, and premium services while keeping core research open.
